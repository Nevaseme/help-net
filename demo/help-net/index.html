Add to your Home Screen
  1. Open Help Net using the link above.
  2. Open your browser’s Share menu. In Safari, you may need to open More first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  4. Open Help Net from your Home Screen and join the group.

Can’t find Add to Home Screen? Copy the link below, open Safari, and paste it into the address bar.
